Our Story

Started Right Here in Victor, NY

TJ's Property Management was founded by two hardworking, college-bound students who grew up in the Victor, NY area. With real landscaping experience and a drive to do right by every customer, we started this business to bring quality outdoor work to our community at prices that make sense.

We're not a faceless crew — we're your neighbors. We show up personally to every job, treat every yard like it's our own, and won't leave until you're happy with the results. Simple as that.
